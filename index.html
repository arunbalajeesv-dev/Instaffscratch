Meet the Fixers

At Shyft On, our working professionals are called Fixers.

Fixer (noun): a trained professional on the Shyft On network who takes a shift at a nearby business and fixes the gap in its roster.

  • Trained for a specific role, such as cashier, kitchen prep, packing or cleaning.
  • Matched by distance, so a Fixer is usually close to your business.
  • Booked by the shift, and you pay only for the shifts you book.

The problem

You pay a full month for a shift you only need twelve days.

Take a café with 8 staff: 3 in the morning, 3 in the evening and 2 on the mid shift. On weekdays the mid shift has little to do. It matters on weekends, and on the one weekday a week when someone is on week off.
